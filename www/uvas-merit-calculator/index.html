How to calculate UVAS aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the Undergraduate formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 30% = 27.82
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 70% = 62.36

Aggregate = 90.18%

UVAS merit calculator FAQs

What is the UVAS merit formula for 2026?

For Undergraduate: 30% Matric + 70% Inter.

Does UVAS have an entry test?

No. Admission is based on academic marks only, so your Matric and Inter marks decide your merit.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including UVAS, use Part-I marks for admission.

Is this UVAS merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with UVAS.

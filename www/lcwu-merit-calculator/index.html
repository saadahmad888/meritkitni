LCWU merit calculator FAQs

What is the LCWU merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 100% Inter.

Does LCWU have an entry test?

No. Admission is based on academic marks only, so your Matric and Inter marks decide your merit.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including LCWU, use Part-I marks for admission.

Is this LCWU merit calculator official?

Lahore College for Women University doesn’t publish its exact merit weightage for these programmes, so the calculator uses the common pattern used by similar universities and marks it as an estimate. In the full app you can adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with LCWU.

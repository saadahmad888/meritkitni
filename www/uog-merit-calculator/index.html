Computing (CS, IT, SE): 100% Inter Part-I (or first two DAE years). No entry test.

Other BS programmes: 50% Inter and 50% UOG admission test. Some programmes add an interview, so check your programme’s notice.

UOG merit calculator FAQs

What is the UOG merit formula for 2026?

For Computing (CS, IT, SE): 100% Inter. For Engineering: 17% Matric + 50% Inter + 33% Engineering entry test. For Other BS programmes: 50% Inter + 50% UOG admission test.

Does UOG have an entry test?

It depends on the programme. Engineering, Other BS programmes use Engineering entry test / UOG admission test, while Computing (CS, IT, SE) has no entry test.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including UOG, use Part-I marks for admission.

Is this UOG merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by University of Gujrat, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with UOG.

What Engineering entry test score do I need for 80% at UOG?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 239 out of 400 in Engineering entry test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

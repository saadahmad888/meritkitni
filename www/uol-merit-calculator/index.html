Engineering: Pakistan Engineering Council rules require at least 33% entry test and 50% Inter for engineering. The remaining 17% is shown as Matric.

The University of Lahore doesn’t publish exact merit weights for programmes marked Estimate. The calculator uses the common pattern for similar universities; confirm with the admission office.

How to calculate UOL aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS programmes formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in UOL entry test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. UOL entry test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

UOL merit calculator FAQs

What is the UOL merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% UOL entry test. For Engineering: 17% Matric + 50% Inter + 33% Engineering entry test.

Does UOL have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (Engineering entry test / UOL entry test) carries 33% / 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including UOL, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this UOL merit calculator official?

The University of Lahore doesn’t publish its exact merit weightage for these programmes, so the calculator uses the common pattern used by similar universities and marks it as an estimate. In the full app you can adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with UOL.

What UOL entry test score do I need for 80% at UOL?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 71 out of 100 in UOL entry test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

FAQ

Questions students ask

Is Merit Calculator free?

Yes. The app and the web version are completely free, with no ads and no sign-up.

How accurate are the results?

Formulas marked Official come from the university’s own admission notices and were checked in October 2026. Universities can change formulas between seasons, so always confirm on the official admission page before applying. Results are estimates and don’t guarantee admission.

What does “Estimate” mean?

Some private universities admit on academics plus their own test but don’t publish the exact weights. For these, the app uses a common pattern and lets you adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers.

My Inter Part-II result isn’t out yet. Can I still use it?

Yes. Enter your Part-I marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Most universities use Part-I marks for admission anyway.

Do you save my marks?

Your marks are saved only on your own device so you don’t have to type them again. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use it on iPhone?

Yes. Open the web app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It works like an app, even offline.

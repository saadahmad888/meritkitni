BS with entry test (CS, IT…): PU reserves 25% for its written entrance test (2024 and 2025 notices). Academic record uses PU’s basic formula: ¼ of Matric plus Inter. Hafiz-e-Quran gets 20 extra marks. A few programmes may use different weights, so check your programme’s page.

BS without entry test: PU’s basic formula: (¼ Matric + Inter + additional marks) ÷ (¼ Matric total + Inter total).

BS CS & IT: Same PU formula as Lahore: 75% academic record, 25% entry test. Hafiz-e-Quran gets 20 extra marks.

BS without entry test: PU’s basic formula: (¼ Matric + Inter + additional marks) ÷ (¼ Matric total + Inter total).

How to calculate PU aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS with entry test (CS, IT…) formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in PU entry test:

  1. Academic record = (¼ × 1020 + 980) ÷ (¼ × 1100 + 1100) = 89.82%
  2. 89.82% × 75% = 67.36
  3. PU entry test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 25% = 17.5

Aggregate = 84.86%

PU merit calculator FAQs

What is the PU merit formula for 2026?

For BS with entry test (CS, IT…): 75% Academic record (¼ Matric + Inter) + 25% PU entry test. For BS without entry test: 100% Academic record (¼ Matric + Inter). For BS CS & IT: 75% Academic record (¼ Matric + Inter) + 25% PU entry test. For BS without entry test: 100% Academic record (¼ Matric + Inter).

Does PU have an entry test?

It depends on the programme. BS with entry test (CS, IT…), BS CS & IT use PU entry test, while BS without entry test, BS without entry test have no entry test.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including PU, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this PU merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by Punjab University, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with PU.

What PU entry test score do I need for 80% at PU?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 51 out of 100 in PU entry test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

How to calculate ITU aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the CS & AI formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in ITU admission test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 15% = 13.91
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 35% = 31.18
  3. ITU admission test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 80.09%

ITU merit calculator FAQs

What is the ITU merit formula for 2026?

For CS & AI: 15% Matric + 35% Inter + 50% ITU admission test.

Does ITU have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (ITU admission test) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including ITU, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this ITU merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by Information Technology University, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with ITU.

What ITU admission test score do I need for 80% at ITU?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 70 out of 100 in ITU admission test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

How to calculate UET aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the Engineering (ECAT) formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 280/400 in ECAT:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 17% = 15.76
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 50% = 44.55
  3. ECAT: 280 ÷ 400 = 70% × 33% = 23.1

Aggregate = 83.41%

UET merit calculator FAQs

What is the UET merit formula for 2026?

For Engineering (ECAT): 17% Matric + 50% Inter + 33% ECAT. For Engineering (ECAT): 17% Matric + 50% Inter + 33% ECAT.

Does UET have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (ECAT) carries 33% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including UET, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this UET merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by UET Lahore, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with UET.

What ECAT score do I need for 80% at UET?

How to calculate GIMS aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS programmes formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in Arid / GIMS entry test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. Arid / GIMS entry test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

GIMS merit calculator FAQs

What is the GIMS merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% Arid / GIMS entry test.

Does GIMS have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (Arid / GIMS entry test) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including GIMS, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this GIMS merit calculator official?

PMAS-Arid University / GIMS Gujrat doesn’t publish its exact merit weightage for these programmes, so the calculator uses the common pattern used by similar universities and marks it as an estimate. In the full app you can adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with GIMS.

What Arid / GIMS entry test score do I need for 80% at GIMS?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 71 out of 100 in Arid / GIMS entry test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

How to calculate MDCAT aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the MBBS / BDS (KEMU, AIMC, SIMS, FJMU…) formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 126/180 in MDCAT:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. MDCAT: 126 ÷ 180 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

MDCAT merit calculator FAQs

What is the MDCAT merit formula for 2026?

For MBBS / BDS (KEMU, AIMC, SIMS, FJMU…): 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% MDCAT. For MBBS: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% MDCAT. For MBBS: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% MDCAT.

Does MDCAT have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (MDCAT) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including MDCAT, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this MDCAT merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by MBBS & BDS admissions, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with MDCAT.

What MDCAT score do I need for 80% at MDCAT?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 127 out of 180 in MDCAT for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

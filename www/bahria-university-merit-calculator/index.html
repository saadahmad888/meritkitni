How to calculate Bahria aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the Undergraduate formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in Bahria test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 30% = 26.73
  3. Bahria test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 60% = 42

Aggregate = 78%

Bahria merit calculator FAQs

What is the Bahria merit formula for 2026?

For Undergraduate: 10% Matric + 30% Inter + 60% Bahria test.

Does Bahria have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (Bahria test) carries 60% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including Bahria, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this Bahria merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by Bahria University, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with Bahria.

What Bahria test score do I need for 80% at Bahria?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 74 out of 100 in Bahria test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

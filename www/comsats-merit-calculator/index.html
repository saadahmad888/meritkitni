How to calculate COMSATS aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS programmes formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in NTS NAT:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. NTS NAT: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

COMSATS merit calculator FAQs

What is the COMSATS merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% NTS NAT.

Does COMSATS have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (NTS NAT) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including COMSATS, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this COMSATS merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by COMSATS University, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with COMSATS.

What NTS NAT score do I need for 80% at COMSATS?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 71 out of 100 in NTS NAT for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

How to calculate Superior aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS programmes formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in Superior entry test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. Superior entry test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

Superior merit calculator FAQs

What is the Superior merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% Superior entry test.

Does Superior have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (Superior entry test) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including Superior, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this Superior merit calculator official?

Superior University Lahore doesn’t publish its exact merit weightage for these programmes, so the calculator uses the common pattern used by similar universities and marks it as an estimate. In the full app you can adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with Superior.

What Superior entry test score do I need for 80% at Superior?

How to calculate GIKI aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the Engineering & computing formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in GIKI admission test:

  1. Academics = average of 92.73% and 89.09% = 90.91% × 15% = 13.64
  2. GIKI admission test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 85% = 59.5

Aggregate = 73.14%

GIKI merit calculator FAQs

What is the GIKI merit formula for 2026?

For Engineering & computing: 15% Matric + Inter Part-I (average) + 85% GIKI admission test.

Does GIKI have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (GIKI admission test) carries 85% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including GIKI, use Part-I marks for admission.

Is this GIKI merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by GIK Institute, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with GIKI.

What GIKI admission test score do I need for 80% at GIKI?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 79 out of 100 in GIKI admission test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

How to calculate NUST aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the Engineering & computing formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 140/200 in NUST NET:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 15% = 13.36
  3. NUST NET: 140 ÷ 200 = 70% × 75% = 52.5

Aggregate = 75.14%

NUST merit calculator FAQs

What is the NUST merit formula for 2026?

For Engineering & computing: 10% Matric + 15% Inter + 75% NUST NET.

Does NUST have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (NUST NET) carries 75% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including NUST, use Part-I marks for admission.

Is this NUST merit calculator accurate?

It uses the formula published by NUST, checked in October 2026. Universities can update formulas between admission cycles, so confirm on the official admission page before applying. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with NUST.

What NUST NET score do I need for 80% at NUST?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 153 out of 200 in NUST NET for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.

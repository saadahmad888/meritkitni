How to calculate University of Chenab aggregate: worked example

Here’s how the BS programmes formula works for a student with 1020/1100 in Matric, 980/1100 in Inter and 70/100 in Chenab entry test:

  1. Matric: 1020 ÷ 1100 = 92.73% × 10% = 9.27
  2. Inter: 980 ÷ 1100 = 89.09% × 40% = 35.64
  3. Chenab entry test: 70 ÷ 100 = 70% × 50% = 35

Aggregate = 79.91%

University of Chenab merit calculator FAQs

What is the University of Chenab merit formula for 2026?

For BS programmes: 10% Matric + 40% Inter + 50% Chenab entry test.

Does University of Chenab have an entry test?

Yes. The entry test (Chenab entry test) carries 50% of the aggregate.

Can I use Inter Part-I marks if my Part-II result isn’t out?

Yes. Enter your Part-I obtained marks and Part-I total (for example 480 out of 550). Many universities, including University of Chenab, accept Part-I marks while results are awaited.

Is this University of Chenab merit calculator official?

University of Chenab Gujrat doesn’t publish its exact merit weightage for these programmes, so the calculator uses the common pattern used by similar universities and marks it as an estimate. In the full app you can adjust the weights if the university tells you different numbers. Merit Calculator isn’t affiliated with University of Chenab.

What Chenab entry test score do I need for 80% at University of Chenab?

With 1020/1100 in Matric and 980/1100 in Inter, you need about 71 out of 100 in Chenab entry test for an 80% aggregate. Enter your own marks above, or use the target finder in the full app.
